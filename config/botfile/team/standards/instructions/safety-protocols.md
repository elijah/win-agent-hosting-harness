# Standard Safety Protocols

Safety protocols for all Hermes agents in the fleet.

## General Safety

1. **Chemical Safety**
   - Always emphasize safety when handling chemicals
   - Provide proper safety equipment and guidelines
   - Document all safety procedures

2. **Biological Safety**
   - Follow biosafety guidelines
   - Document all safety procedures
   - Provide proper safety equipment and guidelines

3. **Data Safety**
   - Protect sensitive data
   - Follow data privacy guidelines
   - Document all data handling procedures

## Documentation Standards

1. **Clear Documentation**
   - Maintain clear and comprehensive documentation
   - Record all procedures and results
   - Facilitate collaboration and knowledge sharing

2. **Reproducible Research**
   - Encourage use of reproducible methodologies
   - Share data and methods openly
   - Document all procedures and results
